## What Is This?

An **AI-powered chatbot** embedded directly on the TechBrahmand website. Instead of clients filling out a static contact form and waiting for a response, they have a real-time conversation with an intelligent assistant that:

1. **Understands their project needs** — asks smart follow-up questions (What kind of app? How many users? Do you need payments? etc.)
2. **Generates a detailed budget** — breaks down the cost module by module (Design: ₹X, Backend: ₹Y, etc.)
3. **Explains the pricing** — tells the client *why* each module costs what it does
4. **Handles pushback** — if a client says "that's too expensive," it suggests phased approaches or adjusted scope

Essentially — a **24/7 digital sales consultant** that qualifies leads and gives instant, professional estimates on behalf of TechBrahmand.

## How Will It Work?

I'll use the powerful **Grok API** and configure it specifically with TechBrahmand's context — your past projects, pricing structure, service offerings (Brahma / Vishnu / Mahesh), and how you communicate with clients.

By setting up a customized system architecture using Grok's lightning-fast model, the AI will respond like a TechBrahmand team member — not a generic chatbot.

Instead of training an open-source model from scratch, we will leverage Grok's advanced API, optimizing it with a robust system context. This avoids heavy compute costs while ensuring exceptionally smart and accurate responses.

| Item | Detail |

### Training Data — The Key Ingredient

The AI learns from **example conversations**. I'll prepare 200–500 sample conversations that teach the bot how to:

- Ask the right questions for different project types (websites, apps, SaaS, maintenance, SEO)
- Calculate and present budgets in TechBrahmand's format
- Represent TechBrahmand's brand voice and professionalism
- Route inquiries to the right service pillar (Brahma / Vishnu / Mahesh)

These examples will be based on **real project data** from TechBrahmand — anonymized where needed.
